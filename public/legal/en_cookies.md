## 1. What are cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on a user's device when accessing certain websites to enable site functionality or remember information about the visit.

## 2. Cookies used on this website
Currently, this website does not use tracking, analytical, or advertising cookies.

Browsing this portfolio does not involve the installation of cookies that collect personal or behavioral data. Only essential technical cookies might be used for the stability and security of the technical infrastructure (Vercel), which do not require prior consent as they are necessary for the service to function.

## 3. Third-party cookies
This website includes links to external platforms (LinkedIn, GitHub, etc.). By clicking these links and leaving our site, the user becomes subject to the cookie policies of those platforms. The owner of this site has no control over the cookies that these third parties may install.

## 4. Updates
Should tools requiring the use of cookies (such as detailed analytics) be incorporated in the future, the user will be properly informed, and a configuration panel will be enabled in compliance with current regulations.
